      How to rate

      Try to play the tune from memory. No peeking. Then rate your recall:

      • 1 Can't remember it
      • 2 Learned it before, forgot it
      • 3 Stumble through, forget parts
      • 4 Can play with effort
      • 5 Flows naturally

      1–4: work on the parts you forgot. The tune comes back tomorrow.

      5: it rests for two days. Keep scoring 5s and the rest gets longer: 2, 4, 8, then 16 days.

      Your tunes are saved in this browser, on this device. They won't follow you to a different phone or browser. Save a backup now and then.
